# Code of Conduct

This project is for people who do product marketing and go-to-market work. Be useful and be kind.

## What I expect

- Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Share real examples without naming customers, colleagues or employers who have not agreed to it.
- Assume the other person is busy and trying to help.

## What I will not accept

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Pasting a customer's confidential data into an issue or pull request.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately through **Security** > **Report a vulnerability** on this repository, or by direct message on the author's Substack. I can remove comments, close issues and block people who break these rules.

This applies in issues, pull requests and discussions in this repository.
