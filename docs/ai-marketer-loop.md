# The AI marketer loop

Your brain is a teammate that knows your company. This page shows the weekly loop that keeps it useful. Every step is yours to approve.

## The loop

1. **Know** — your brain holds the company: product, buyer, alternatives, voice, market, proof, strategy, and how work gets done (Sections 1 to 8). Build it from your website and material with `/product-marketing-context:build-brain`.
2. **Notice** — once a week, `/pmm-intelligence:signal-monitor` reads the signals you name and returns at most five opportunities. Each is tied to a brain field and to a Section 7 priority.
3. **Decide** — you pick which, if any, to act on. Nothing runs without you.
4. **Act** — the list points at one skill per opportunity, such as `alternatives-map`, `positioning-messaging` or `pmm-okrs`. Each asks before it changes anything.
5. **Learn** — when the work shows the brain was wrong or stale, `/product-marketing-context:refresh-brain` and `/product-marketing-context:brain-gaps` fix it, one question at a time.

## Set up the weekly read

Pick a time, then ask Claude:

> Create a scheduled task that runs every Monday at 9:00. It should run signal-monitor on these sources: [list the pages, folders or connectors you want read]. It must save the list to /context/signals/ and must not change my brain.

A scheduled task starts a fresh session, so give it access to the folder that holds your brain and name every source it may read. A source you do not name is not read.

## What to expect

- Most weeks, two or three opportunities. A week with none is a good result.
- Each opportunity shows its evidence. If you cannot find the evidence, do not act on it.
- The list is read-only. It never edits your brain.
- If you act on one and learn something, tell the brain. That is the step that makes next week's read better.

## What it is not

It is not autonomous. It does not publish, send or change anything on its own. It reads what you name, ranks it against what you said matters, and waits for you.
