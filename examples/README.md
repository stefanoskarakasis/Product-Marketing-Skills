# Examples

## Try the skills without building a brain

`example-brain.md` is a finished brain for **Ledgerlane**, a fictional accounts payable company. Use it to see what the skills produce before you spend 15 minutes on your own.

Everything in it is invented: the company, the customers, the numbers and the quotes. Do not cite any of it.

### How to use it

1. Open `example-brain.md` and copy the whole file.
2. Put it where the skills look:
   - **Claude Code or Cowork with a folder:** save it as `/foundation/brain.md` in your working folder.
   - **Claude.ai chat or a Project:** paste it into the chat, or add it to Project knowledge, and say "Use this as my brain."
3. Run one of the prompts below.

### Five prompts to try

| Plugin | Prompt | What you should see |
|---|---|---|
| `pmm-positioning` | "Build our message house." | A Roof and Pillars table filled from the brain, with anything missing listed under "Still needed". |
| `pmm-positioning` | "Build a messaging playbook for our new exception-routing feature." | A playbook with story, one competitor comparison and objection handling, with unsourced claims flagged. |
| `pmm-growth` | "Give me positioning angles to choose between." | Several divergent angles, each tied to a named alternative from Section 3. |
| `pmm-growth` | "I have a call with Harbor & Pine Freight on Thursday. What do I ask?" | Interview questions trimmed to what Section 6 already proves. |
| `pmm-go-to-market` | "What launch tier is exception routing?" | A tier (T1 to T4) with the signals behind it. |

### Then switch to your own

Delete the example brain, then run `/product-marketing-context:build-brain`. Or start with `alternatives-map`, `ideal-customer-profile` or `proof-points` in `pmm-positioning`: each works from your own notes and starts your brain with its section.
