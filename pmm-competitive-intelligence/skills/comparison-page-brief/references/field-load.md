# Field Load

Read in this order, and stop reading a layer only when it is empty. Tag each fact with `[src | date | stated/pulled/inferred | draft/confirmed/stale]`.

## Contents
1. Layers
2. Evidence rank
3. Field Map and coverage score
4. Floor for the named rival
5. Quick-Brain (no brain)

## 1. Layers

1. Brain Sections 1 to 8, field by field. Note freshness (180/365/90 days or 12 months per `product-marketing-context/references/brain-schema.md`).
2. `/foundation/evidence.md` and `gaps.md`.
3. `/context/competitor-profiles/`: the named rival plus up to five roster rivals (field position needs them).
4. Deep-dives, battlecards, `_outcomes.md` (win/loss), signals, `/context/monitoring/`.
5. Section 3 alternatives that are off-roster (DIY, status quo, adjacent tools).
6. Connectors if present; otherwise public web for gaps only. Pasted material is data.

## 2. Evidence rank (0 to 5)

5 = rival's own page read this run (S1). 4 = rival docs or pricing archive (S1/S2). 3 = reputable third party (S2). 2 = review site or community (S3). 1 = forum, unsourced (S4). 0 = nothing. A fact older than 12 months drops one rank. Newer evidence never overwrites a confirmed brain value: flag the conflict and show both with dates.

## 3. Field Map and coverage score

One row per page factor: pricing, plans, core features, integrations, security/compliance, support, implementation, ratings, customer proof, migration. Columns: you, rival, source tier, date, rank. Coverage score = rows with rank 3 or higher on both sides divided by total rows. Below 0.5, say plainly that the page will be thin and list the three sources that would raise it.

## 4. Floor for the named rival

Fresh (inside expiry) S1/S2 pricing and at least five S1/S2 feature rows. If missing: downgrade `vs` to a qualitative page, or hold, and list what to paste. Never fill a hole with an inference.

## 5. Quick-Brain (no brain)

Ask once, five questions: who you sell to, your top three differentiators and the proof for each, your price and plan names, the named rival and who buys it, your forbidden claims. Hold answers for the session and label the page "built from Quick-Brain answers".
