# Gotchas

Reference for `weekly-competitive-updates`.

## Gotchas

- **A quiet week is a result, not a failure.** Do not pad it with background. The Coverage line and Data Confidence & Gaps are what make "nothing happened" believable.
- **The boundary day is reported twice.** An item on the last day of one window is also the first day of the next. The Items table of the last edition is how it is caught; check it before writing.
- **A lead is not a source.** A row in `signal-log.md` or a profile says where to look. If the page cannot be retrieved this run, the item is ⚠️ with "link pending" or it is out.
- **A stale requester in a scheduled run is an assumption.** Past 90 days, or never confirmed at scheduling, the run goes cross-market and says so in the Coverage line. It does not borrow an identity from the brain or from `me.md`.
- **A weak signal that never resolves is noise.** If a watchlist item lapses, drop it and say so once. Three lapses in a row is a pattern for the Learning Close.
- **A competitor's own page proves only that it published it.** A company's own announcement of its own price, launch or partnership is a primary fact: use it with ⚠️ self-published. Its claims about results, customers or market position (its own metrics) go to Data Confidence & Gaps, not into a takeaway as fact.
- **An ambiguous date is not "this week".** "Last week" in an article means nothing without the article's date. Mark ⚠️ or leave it out.
- **Scheduled runs are fresh sessions.** Continuity lives in `/context/weekly-updates/` or the Carry-forward Block. Without either, say continuity is limited; do not guess last week's levels.
