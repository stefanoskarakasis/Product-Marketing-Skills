# /pmm-execution:stakeholder-map -- Stakeholder Map

Build a political stakeholder map that identifies who can champion, who
can block, and what to say to each person before a launch or initiative
moves forward.

## Invocation

```
/pmm-execution:stakeholder-map SSO launch for enterprise customers
/pmm-execution:stakeholder-map Q3 pricing change rollout
```

## Workflow

Uses the `stakeholder-maps` skill. Runs intake on the initiative, maps
each stakeholder by power and interest, assigns a political role
(champion, blocker, frozen, gatekeeper), and produces a communication
plan with a specific message for each high-priority stakeholder.
