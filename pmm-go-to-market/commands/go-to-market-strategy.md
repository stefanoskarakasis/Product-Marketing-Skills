# /pmm-go-to-market:go-to-market-strategy -- GTM Strategy

Assign a launch tier (T1-T4) and generate a complete strategy brief —
positioning angles, channel strategy, success metrics, and competitive
context.

## Invocation

```
/pmm-go-to-market:go-to-market-strategy SSO integration for enterprise
/pmm-go-to-market:go-to-market-strategy What tier is this pricing change?
```

## Workflow

Uses the `go-to-market-strategy` skill. Assigns tier based on scope and
stakes, generates the strategy brief, and self-learns from past launch
history to make future tier recommendations sharper.
