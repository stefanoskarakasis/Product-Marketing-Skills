# Customer review pack

Read in Step 8. The skill drafts these; the writer sends them. Every `[PLACEHOLDER]` is filled from the ledger or asked for. Never guess a name, department or date.

## What goes in the pack

1. The near-final story, with every `[NEEDS APPROVAL]` still visible.
2. A cover note to the customer contact.
3. A note for whoever owns the customer relationship, asking them to make the introduction or send the pack.
4. A checklist for the customer: every number, quote, visual and permitted use in the story.
5. The asset request list.

## Cover note to the customer

```
Subject: Your [PRODUCT] story, ready for your review

Hi [CONTACT FIRST NAME],

Thank you for the time you gave us on [CALL DATE]. Attached is the story we wrote
from it. It is close to final, and nothing runs without your approval.

Please check:
- Every number and its source
- Each quote, word for word, with your name and title as shown
- The images and logo
- That the uses listed below are ones you are happy with

Permitted uses we are asking about: [LIST FROM LEDGER]

If you can reply by [DATE], we can publish on [PUBLISH DATE]. Anything you want
changed, tell us and we will change it.

[SENDER NAME]
```

## Note for the relationship owner

Send to the person named in the ledger as customer contact owner. If that role is "not set", ask the writer who it is before drafting.

```
Hi [NAME],

We are ready to send [CUSTOMER] their story for review. Could you [introduce us to
/ forward the pack to] [CONTACT NAME, TITLE]? What helps: a short heads-up before
it lands, and a nudge if we have not heard by [DATE]. The pack is attached.

[SENDER NAME]
```

## Customer checklist

| Item | In the story | Customer confirms |
|---|---|---|
| Stat: [value, baseline, period] | [where it appears] | ☐ |
| Quote: "[first words]" | [speaker, title] | ☐ |
| Visual or logo | [which] | ☐ |
| Use: [permitted use] | | ☐ |

One row per stat, quote, visual and use. A row the customer does not tick stays `[NEEDS APPROVAL]`.

## Asset request list

Ask for: desktop and mobile screenshots, GIFs of the product in use, logo (and usage rules), a headshot for each quoted person, and any visuals they already approved. Add a line for what the customer may not share.

## After feedback

Apply each change, re-run the gate, update stats and quotes in the ledger, set `approved` only when the customer and every internal approver in Setup have confirmed.
