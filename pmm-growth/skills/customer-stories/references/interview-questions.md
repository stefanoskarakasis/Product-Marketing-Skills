# Interview questions

Twelve questions for a 30 to 45 minute customer call. Trim to the gaps already visible in the notes. The five marked **block** stop publication if unanswered.

## Before the call

Pull these first and mark them unverified. Share the list with the writer so nothing is asked twice.

- **Survey comments.** Open-text answers from the customer's NPS or satisfaction survey. Treat each as a quote candidate: it needs the speaker's name, title and approval before it can run. Read it back on the call.
- **Call recordings** and **CRM notes** for the account: usage, expansion, champions.
- **Support history** and any public mention.

## How to ask

- Ask for the number first, then the story around it. People remember the story and forget the figure.
- Ask "what did that cost you" and "what did you do instead" to get a pain quote in the customer's words.
- When an answer is vague ("much better", "a lot faster"), ask for the before and after and the unit.
- Ask question 8 even if the customer is happy. The honest limitation is usually the most credible passage in the finished story.
- Ask question 12 near the end. The answer often shows why they keep choosing you and what they would expand.
- If a result is "up to X", ask what a typical customer or team sees.
- Read quotes back for confirmation before the call ends. Verbatim, with name and title.
- Do not read the cannot-say list aloud, but steer around it.
