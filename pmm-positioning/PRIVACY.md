# Privacy Policy: pmm-positioning

Last updated: 7 November 2026

`pmm-positioning` is a Claude plugin made of skill instructions and slash commands. It is
published by Stefanos Karakasis ("the author").

## What the author collects

Nothing. The plugin runs inside your own Claude session. It has no servers, no analytics, no
telemetry and no accounts, and the author never receives your prompts, files, company
information or outputs.

## What the plugin reads and writes

- Files and text you give Claude during a session, such as research notes, call transcripts,
  decks and any brain files you choose to point it at. They are processed by Claude under the
  terms and privacy policy of your Claude plan.
- Output the skills create (for example an ICP, an alternatives map or a messaging playbook) is
  saved only where you or your Claude session save it.

## Third-party connectors

The plugin includes an `.mcp.json` that lists optional connector servers for tools such as
Notion, Slack, HubSpot, Gong and Google Workspace. Each connector is off until you connect it
and sign in yourself. When you connect one, data moves between Claude and that vendor under the
vendor's own terms and privacy policy. The author does not receive that data and does not
operate those servers.

## Your choices

You can decline or disconnect any connector at any time, uninstall the plugin, and delete
anything the skills saved on your side.
