# /pmm-positioning:alternatives-map -- Alternatives Map

Maps who buyers actually compare you to across all four real alternative types — direct competitors, adjacent tools, DIY builds, and the status quo — from win/loss notes, call transcripts, or reviews, since teams that only track named competitors miss the alternative that wins the most deals. Use it before running positioning or messaging work, since both hard-block without a real, evidenced alternatives map in place.

## Invocation

```
/pmm-positioning:alternatives-map Map our alternatives from these 6 win/loss notes
/pmm-positioning:alternatives-map Here are 10 sales call transcripts — who do we compete with
/pmm-positioning:alternatives-map Our alternatives section just says "legacy tools"
```

## Workflow

Uses the `alternatives-map` skill. Loads brain Section 3 if present,
gathers and names real alternatives across all four types (status quo is
never optional — it wins the majority of B2B deals in most markets), maps
each across why-chosen, why-left, and unclaimed territory with sources
cited and assumptions flagged `[A]`, runs the Named-Not-Abstract exit
check and a gap-credibility test, then deepens brain Section 3 in place
on explicit confirmation. Feeds directly into `positioning-ideas`
(divergent options) and `positioning-messaging` (shippable copy), both of
which hard-block without a real Section 3.
