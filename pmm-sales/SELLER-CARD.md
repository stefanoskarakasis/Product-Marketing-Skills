# Seller card: prospect-research

Pin this in your sales channel. Time to read: 2 minutes.

## What you get
- 🟢 🟡 🔴 **A verdict** on one company, in the thread, in about 120 words.
- 🎯 **A reason, an angle and a first question** you can use on your next call or message.
- 🚫 **A "why not now"** when the answer is wait or no.

## Ask it
- `@Claude is there a reason to contact [company] now? I'm going to their [role].`
- `@Claude what changed at [company] since my last brief?`
- `log: meeting booked at [company]` (one line, so the team learns what works)

## Before your first ask
- [ ] Your admin has connected your CRM and shared drive. Not sure? Ask in this channel.
- [ ] You are in an internal channel with colleagues only.

## Never
- ❌ Paste a customer's private data, contract terms or contact details into the thread.
- ❌ Treat the hypothesis as fact. It is labelled inference for a reason.
- ❌ Send anything the skill wrote. It writes none; you do.

## If the answer looks wrong
Reply in the thread with what is wrong. Your PMM sees it and fixes the brain.

## Europe
For companies in Europe the skill gives role titles only and no contact details. Check outreach rules for the country before you sequence.
