# /pmm-sales:objection-handling -- Objection Handling

Tells you what a buyer objection really is, gives the next line to say, a call digest, or a paste-ready email reply, and logs it so the objection library gets sharper every week. Diagnoses before it answers, never concedes in a first reply, never invents a figure or quotes a rival's price. Draft only; nothing is sent.

## Invocation

```
/pmm-sales:objection-handling They said it's too expensive, 5% is a lot
/pmm-sales:objection-handling Digest the Marlow call (transcript pasted)
/pmm-sales:objection-handling Draft a reply to this email
/pmm-sales:objection-handling Prep me for objections on Northwind
/pmm-sales:objection-handling Build the library from our last 20 calls
/pmm-sales:objection-handling Review the objection library
```

In Slack, tag Claude in a thread: "@Claude they said it's too expensive".

## Workflow

Uses the `objection-handling` skill. Loads the brain, your role and the objection library, matches a card, diagnoses the type behind the words, applies the play, proves only with sourced facts, writes the shape for your surface, runs a 13-check gate, logs the objection with a one-line receipt, proposes what other skills should learn once enough deals agree, asks who owns objection knowledge when no owner is confirmed, and logs the session. Read-only for your brain, CRM and inbox.
