# Feed-Forward: What Other Skills Learn From Accounts

Read at Step 13. Customer-context sees real customers, so it is the cheapest honest source of buyer language, objections and proof. It never writes the brain. It logs, counts, and proposes.

## Mechanism

1. Each run appends rows to `/context/customers/_patterns.md`: date, account slug, type, segment (from Section 2 terms), role involved, count. No contact names, no amounts, no quotes except customer phrases below.
2. A pattern is proposed only when it appears in two or more accounts inside 180 days. One account stays a note.
3. A proposal is shown as exact text with the receiving skill named. The user confirms, then runs the receiving skill. Unattended runs log and propose nothing.
4. The Learning Close row lets `meta-synthesis` see which proposals were accepted.

## Targets

| What the run observed | n needed | Proposed to | Receiving skill does |
|---|---|---|---|
| A customer phrase for the problem or for us, verbatim, with who said it and when | 1 (verbatim only) | `product-marketing-context` (Add material) | Stores in Section 4 `customer_phrases` after the user's yes; `brand-voice` and `positioning-messaging` then read it |
| An objection heard, raised by which role | 2 accounts | `product-marketing-context` | Adds to Section 3 `objections`; `competitive-battlecard` reads it |
| A named alternative, status quo or DIY not in Section 3 | 2 accounts | `alternatives-map` | Adds it with evidence |
| A reason customers gave for switching to you, or nearly leaving | 2 accounts | `alternatives-map` | Updates `switching_forces` (push, pull, habit, anxiety) |
| A competitor seen in an active renewal or expansion, with the role and the stage | 2 accounts | `competitive-profile` (`intake`) | Drafts one change for the PMM or named approver |
| A measured outcome the customer stated (metric, baseline, period) | 1 | `proof-points` (Add mode) | Registers it as `[NEEDS APPROVAL]` until the customer approves |
| An advocate signal: referral, talked to a prospect, took part in a story | 1 | `customer-stories` | Starts a kickoff if the user wants one |
| Accounts that match `anti_persona`, or a segment outside Section 2 that renews well | 3 accounts | `ideal-customer-profile` (audit) | Pressure-tests the ICP |
| Champion, blocker and economic-buyer roles seen across accounts | 3 accounts | `buyer-personas`, `stakeholder-maps` | Updates `committee_roles` defaults |
| Churn or near-churn themes | 2 accounts | `interview-summary` (churn mode) | Structures the debrief |

## Rules

- Customer phrases are exact quotes with a source, or they are not stored.
- A measured outcome is never used in a brief as proof until `proof-points` has it as approved.
- A proposal that conflicts with a confirmed brain value is shown as a question with both values.
- Nothing here changes a freshness window; `meta-synthesis` proposes that.
