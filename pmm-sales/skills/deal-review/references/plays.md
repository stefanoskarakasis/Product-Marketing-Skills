# Plays and Next Steps

Read at Steps 7 and 8. Match the play to the gap. Never recommend a play for a gap the evidence does not show.

## Next-step shape

At most three. Order: what the buyer is waiting on from us; then anything with a buyer-dated deadline; then the biggest gap. Each: **Action** · **Play** · **Type** (information, champion, escalation, competitive, process, qualification) · **Owner** (a role) · **By** (a date) · **In practice** (the exact move, ask or a sample line) · **Could fail if** (one way). End with "After this review, different:" one changed behaviour or decision. A vague step ("keep in touch") is rewritten or dropped.

## Plays

| Gap | Play | In practice, for example |
|---|---|---|
| Value not quantified | Quantify the value | Ask how they would measure it; collect their numbers; one page tied to save money, make money or reduce risk |
| Nobody senior | Earn senior access | To the champion: "So we don't spend your time on something leadership won't fund, can we get 20 minutes with [role]?" |
| No champion | Build the champion | Find their personal win; give them one line to repeat upward |
| Single thread | Multi-thread safely | Add value first; give a reason; say each person's part in the meeting |
| Risk of change | De-risk | Name security, IT and approval risks before they do; offer a plan and a reference |
| No buying process | Mutual action plan | Draft backward from their dated event; hand to `writing-assistant` |
| "Let me talk internally" | Stay in the room | Ask what concerns the team is raising; offer one more useful next step with a date |
| Price before scope | Scope first | Agree scope before any number; never discount unprompted |
| Cheaper rival | Tie our edge to their priorities | Link our edge to the outcomes they ranked highest; name the risk of missing it |
| Deal not real | Disqualify or downgrade | Say why in one line; close lost or move to nurture; set a revisit date |

Test one next step against walking away and say why it stays or goes.

## Questions

Three parts. **(a) Must answer** (1 to 3): the answers that would most change the deal. **(b) Send now** (email): easy for the buyer to answer in one line. **(c) By category**, only categories the gaps need (priority and cost, decision process and dates, people and access, risk and alternatives): two questions each plus one line on how to weave them into a call.
