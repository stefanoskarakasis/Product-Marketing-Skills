# Question Bank

Read at Step 6 and by `library`. Every question opens with How, What or To what extent, because those get mechanisms, numbers and trade-offs, where "do", "can" and "is" get a yes. Questions are openers: ask, then follow with "what happens next?", "who else?", "how often?" and "what happens when it goes wrong?".

Each row: id, who it fits, the question, and what to listen for. `_questions.md` tracks each id, so the plan can prefer the ones that unlock evidence in your own calls.

Seniority: **IC** practitioner, **M** manager, **D** director or VP, **X** executive. Depth follows the role. An IC gets workflow and daily pain; an executive gets revenue, cost, risk and board priorities. Bridge up with D-03.

## 1. Priority

| Id | Fits | Question | Listen for |
|---|---|---|---|
| P-01 | all | What made this worth a conversation right now? | The real trigger; whether they or someone else asked for the meeting |
| P-02 | all | What did I get wrong, and what would you add? | A corrected priority in their words |
| P-03 | D, X | How would you stack-rank these against everything else on your plate this quarter? | Where this sits; a priority that ranks below three is a "someday" |
| P-04 | all | What is already in motion on this: who owns it, what is funded, what is on the calendar? | A named owner, a budget, a date. "We will get to it" means not active |
| P-05 | IC, M | How does this show up in your week, and what does your manager ask you about it? | Whether it is measured upward |
| P-06 | X | What would your board need to see change by [period] for this to count as a win? | A dated, board-level outcome |

## 2. Current state

| Id | Fits | Question | Listen for |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-01 | all | How are you handling [priority] today, end to end? | Steps, handoffs, tools, owners |
| C-02 | all | What happens next, and who picks it up? | Handoff gaps, hidden people |
| C-03 | all | How often does that run, and how long does it take? | Volume and cycle time to size later |
| C-04 | M, D | What tools and teams touch it today? | The stack and the status quo you replace |
| C-05 | all | What happens when it goes wrong, and how do you find out? | Failure modes; found by a customer or by a dashboard |
| C-06 | IC | What did you do the last time this came up, step by step? | The real workflow, not the documented one |
| C-07 | D, X | How is this measured today, and what does the number look like? | The baseline in their numbers |
| C-08 | all | To what extent does this depend on one person or one spreadsheet? | Key-person and fragility risk |

## 3. Blockers

| Id | Fits | Question | Listen for |
|---|---|---|---|
| B-01 | open buyer | What is getting in the way of moving faster on this? | The friction in their words |
| B-02 | guarded buyer | What part of this creates the most friction for you? | Same, less direct |
| B-03 | all | What have you already tried, and what happened? | Past attempts, why they failed, who is scarred |
| B-04 | M, D | What is stopping this from moving faster: people, budget, tooling or priorities? | The real constraint |
| B-05 | M, D | To what extent is the constraint inside your team versus another team? | Dependencies and who else belongs in the deal |
| B-06 | D | What do you need from [adjacent function] that you do not have today? | Cross-functional blockers |

## 4. Negative consequences

| Id | Fits | Question | Listen for |
|---|---|---|---|
| N-01 | all | What does that cost the business when it happens? | A cost: time, money, customers, risk |
| N-02 | all | What does that stop you from doing? | The opportunity cost |
| N-03 | all | What other roles or teams are affected when it breaks? | The size of the problem and the next stakeholder |
| N-04 | D, X | What happens to [their goal] if this stays as it is for another two quarters? | Urgency in their terms, not yours |
| N-05 | X | How does this show up in the numbers your CFO or board reads? | Budget-grade consequences |
| N-06 | all | What did the last incident cost you: time, money, customers? | A concrete, dated number |
| N-07 | IC | How does this affect the way your own work gets judged? | Personal stake of a champion |

## 5. After state

Share one approved customer story first (Section 6 only), then ask. Buyers are not experts in your space; left alone they picture a basic fix and you compete on price. No approved story: skip the story, write "no approved story" in the plan, and ask the question alone.

| Id | Fits | Question | Listen for |
|---|---|---|---|
| A-01 | all | A customer in [segment] [approved outcome]. What would that look like for your team? | Whether the bigger picture lands |
| A-02 | all | What does better look like in twelve months? | The after state, in their words |
| A-03 | IC, M | How would your day, or your team's week, change? | Daily value, adoption drivers |
| A-04 | M, D | What would you stop doing? | Work removed; the saving |
| A-05 | all | What would have to be true for you to call this solved? | The success definition |
| A-06 | X | How would you describe the end state to your board? | The executive story |

## 6. Positive business outcomes

Use their numbers. A research-based number is shredded in procurement.

| Id | Fits | Question | Listen for |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | all | How will you know it worked: which number moves? | The metric |
| R-02 | M, D, X | What is that number today, and what does it need to be? | Baseline and target |
| R-03 | D, X | How does that number get signed off, and what does the approver look at? | The economic buyer |
| R-04 | D, X | What is that change worth per quarter, in your terms? | Value they own |
| R-05 | all | How do you measure it now, and how reliable is that? | Data they trust |
| R-06 | all | To what extent is that tied to your team's goals, or your own? | Personal and team incentive |

## 7. Requirements and next steps

| Id | Fits | Question | Listen for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q-01 | all | What must be true of a solution for you to move forward? | Requirements. If you can meet all of them easily, so can everyone else: treat that as a red flag, not a win |
| Q-02 | M, D | What does your evaluation look like: who is involved, which steps, by when? | Process, committee, dates |
| Q-03 | D, X | What has to be true for this to get funded this year? | Budget path |
| Q-04 | all | How have you evaluated something like this before, and what happened? | Their pattern and scars |
| Q-05 | all | What would make you rule an option out quickly? | Disqualifiers |
| Q-06 | all | What other roles should be in the next conversation, and what would each want to see? | The next thread |
| Q-07 | all | What is the most useful next step for you, and when? | A dated, mutual next step |

## 8. Differentiation questions

Only for an edge that has a source (brain Section 3, a battlecard, or a cited page). Work backwards: the edge, how the buyer would describe it in their own words, the question that gets them there without your product or your name. Never mention your company or product. An unsourced rival claim becomes a question, never a statement.

- Pattern: "What happens to [priority] if [the thing the edge handles, in their words]?"
- Pattern: "To what extent does it matter that [capability, in their words] works for [their situation]?"
- Pattern: "How important is it that [outcome the edge delivers] holds when [their constraint]?"

Plan wording for the seller: "Where we are different, in their words." Never "trap".

## 9. Bridges and closers

| Id | Question | Use |
|---|---|---|
| D-01 | What would your VP say matters most here? | An IC who answers narrowly: bridge up |
| D-02 | What did I miss that I should have asked? | The close of every call |
| D-03 | What would you want me to understand before we go further? | A guarded buyer |
| D-04 | How would you like to use the rest of our time? | A buyer who is solution-aware and impatient: meet them where they are |

## 10. Fast-disqualify lines

Use when the priority is acknowledged but not actively worked, or the account matches an `anti_persona` or Section 2 disqualifier.

- "If [outcome] is not something you are trying to change this year, I do not think we are a fit, and that is fine. What is the priority instead?"
- "It sounds like this is on the list but not moving. What would have to change for it to move?"
