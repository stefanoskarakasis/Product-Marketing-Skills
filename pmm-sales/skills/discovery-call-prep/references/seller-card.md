# Seller card: discovery-call-prep

Pin this in your sales channel. Time to read: 2 minutes.

## What you get
- 🎯 **A bet on the buyer's priority**, in the thread, in about 150 words.
- ❓ **Three questions to ask**, and what confirms or kills the bet.
- 🏁 **A fast no** when the account is not a fit.
- 📄 **A full plan** in a Doc or chat when you want the seven stages.

## Ask it
- `@Claude prep my discovery call with the [title] at [company], 30 min`
- `@Claude next discovery call with [company]` (paste or upload the last transcript)
- `log: confirmed` / `log: changed: [their priority]` / `log: not a fit` (optional)

## Your routine
1. The day before: run prospect research, then ask for the prep. Paste the invite note.
2. Before the call: read the card only. Say the bet out loud.
3. On the call: open with your guess and ask what you got wrong. Drop the bet at ten minutes if it is dead.
4. After: nothing is required. To see what you missed, paste the transcript into `discovery-call-scoring`. A transcript from your recorder teaches it more than a typed reply.

## Before your first ask
- [ ] Your owner has decided whether Claude may read recorded calls and CRM outcomes. Until then it uses only what you paste.
- [ ] You are in an internal channel with colleagues only.

## Never
- ❌ Paste a customer's private data, contract terms or contact details into the thread.
- ❌ Read the bet as a fact. It is labelled inference.
- ❌ Send anything the skill wrote. It writes drafts; you decide.

## If the answer looks wrong
Reply in the thread with what is wrong. Your owner sees it and fixes the brain or the question bank.
