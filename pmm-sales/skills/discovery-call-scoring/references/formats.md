# Formats

Read at Step 9. Pick the surface first; never ask the seller which format they want.

## Slack (at most 180 words)

The reply is the card only: no run notes below it. Assumptions go on the Sources line. Count words before sending; cut the least useful line first. A bold title, then a bold header with one emoji for every section. Under each header, bullets only, one sentence per bullet. No tables. Roles only. Buyer words paraphrased, never quoted, in a thread others can see. In a channel with people outside the company, stop and say so. Ends on a plain question.

```
**🎙️ Call review · [role] · [first / follow-up]**

**🎯 The read**
• Pattern: [name].
• Strength: [one, with where it showed].
• Biggest lever: [one].

**📊 Scorecard**
• Point of view: [mark] [3 to 6 words].
• Priorities: [mark] [3 to 6 words].
• Current state: [mark] [3 to 6 words].
• Blockers: [mark] [3 to 6 words].
• Negative consequences: [mark] [3 to 6 words].
• After state: [mark] [3 to 6 words].
• Business outcomes: [mark] [3 to 6 words].

**🔁 Replay**
• [Moment, then the move, one line.]
• [Second moment, one line.]

**➡️ Next call**
• [Move]. Say: "[line]"
• [Move]. Say: "[line]"

Want the full replay as a Doc?
```

## Full review (Doc, chat or file)

Same headers, same order, every run. Real headers, bold not italics, one fact per bullet, empty sections never print. The scorecard is bullets, never a table. Order, with these exact headers:

1. **🎯 The read:** pattern, strength with the moment, biggest lever.
2. **📊 Scorecard:** seven stages, mark, trajectory, one exact evidence line each; then the technique line. A single call adds: "send me your next call and I will tell you if the so-what is landing".
3. **🔁 Replay:** per moment: the moment, what you did, what was there (line, prep or allocation, typed), what it opens.
4. **Plan versus call:** only with a plan record (no emoji).
5. **🧭 Coaching:** per Developing or Needs Work stage: what happened, why it matters, what follows.
6. **➡️ Next-call moves:** two to four, each with "In practice".
7. **📎 Sources:** transcript, plan, brain fields read, assumptions, mode (EMEA or not).

Paste-ready lines are last and carry no product name, no invented urgency and no unapproved number.
