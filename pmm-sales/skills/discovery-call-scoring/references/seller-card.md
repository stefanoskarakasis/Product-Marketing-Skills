# Seller card: discovery-call-scoring

Pin this in your sales channel. Time to read: 2 minutes.

## What you get
- 🎯 **The read:** the pattern in your call, one strength, one lever.
- 📊 **A scorecard:** seven stages, an honest mark each.
- 🔁 **A replay:** the two to four moments that cost you, and the move that was there.
- ➡️ **Next-call moves:** two to four, with the words to say.

## Ask it
- `@Claude score this discovery call` (paste or upload the transcript)
- `@Claude what did I miss on the [company] call?`
- `@Claude am I getting better?` (reads your earlier reviews)

## Your routine
1. Before the call: ask `discovery-call-prep`. If you have reviewed a call before, the plan opens with what you wanted to fix.
2. Within a day after: paste the transcript, or ask for the call by company.
3. Read the read and the next-call moves. Pick one move.
4. Use it on the next call. Send that transcript and ask "am I getting better?".

## Before your first ask
- [ ] Your owner has decided whether Claude may read recorded calls. Until then it uses only what you paste.
- [ ] You are in an internal channel with colleagues only.

## Private
- 🔒 Only you see your reviews. Your manager does not. Nothing is ranked.
- 🔒 It scores your own calls only. It will not score a colleague's.
- 🔒 Your transcript is not saved. Say "delete my reviews" and your file goes.

## If the marks look wrong
Reply in the thread with what is wrong. Your owner sees it and fixes the brain or the questions.
