# Formats

Draft only. Nothing is sent. No framework labels in output.

## Slack (about 100 words, no headers)

Bold labels, emoji only as section or status markers.

```
💬 **Heard:** "[exact words]" ([role], [stage])
🧭 **Type:** [type behind the words]
🎯 **Say now:** [acknowledge] · [reframe, one proof] · [question that ends the line]
🌍 **Local:** [one line for this buyer role and market, when known]
🧱 **Don't:** [the likely wrong move]
➡️ **Next step:** [action], [owner role], [date]
📎 **Sourced:** [source, or "unverified"]
⚠️ **Not tailored:** [only without a brain: the one field that would change this answer]
❓ [one question that unlocks the next move]
🧾 Logged: [card], [deal], [stage]. Undo?
```

## Phone-call digest (chat or file)

Sized headers. For each objection: timestamp, buyer's words, type, what the seller did, what was missed, a dated next step. Close with the call's one lesson. Non-English quotes keep the original with a bracketed translation.

## Email reply

Sections: **Diagnosis** (two lines), **Draft** (paste-ready), **Why this reply** (one line), **Before you send** (anything unverified). Honest drafts: if the right move is to park the point, draft that.

## Prep

Three most likely objections on the call: the diagnostic question, the proof slot, the line to use, what not to say. Then the readiness check and a 20-second role-play.

## Receipts

One line after every log. No stats in the receipt.
