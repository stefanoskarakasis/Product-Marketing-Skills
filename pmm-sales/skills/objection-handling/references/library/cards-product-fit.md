# Product fit: seed cards

Seed cards. Tailor `[slots]` to your product. A card in this category stays seed until Product has cleared it. Never concede a gap in the first reply; qualify, then route.

Every card: **Heard** (the buyer's words), **Real issue**, **Ask**, **Say**, **Prove**, **Route**. Method: `../method.md`.

## PF-01 Missing feature

- **Heard:** "You don't have [feature]."
- **Real issue:** A real gap, a workaround exists, or the need is not real.
- **Ask:** "What would [feature] let you do that you can't do today?"
- **Say:** "Let me make sure I understand the outcome you need from [feature]. Walk me through how that works for you now."
- **Prove:** `offerings`; roadmap only if approved; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Product via the owner if it repeats
- **Status:** seed

## PF-02 Platform or OS support

- **Heard:** "It doesn't run on [platform]."
- **Real issue:** A hard constraint, or a preference of one team.
- **Ask:** "Which teams need it on [platform], and for what task?"
- **Say:** "Which users would be on [platform], and what would they do in it?"
- **Prove:** `offerings`; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Product via the owner
- **Status:** seed

## PF-03 Customization

- **Heard:** "We need it to fit our process."
- **Real issue:** A genuine process, or a habit of the current tool.
- **Ask:** "Which part of your process is fixed, and which is just how it's done today?"
- **Say:** "Show me the process step that matters most. What has to be exactly that way?"
- **Prove:** `offerings`; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Solutions or Product
- **Status:** seed

## PF-04 Storage or volume limits

- **Heard:** "We have far more data than that."
- **Real issue:** A real volume need, or an assumption.
- **Ask:** "What volume do you expect in year one and year three?"
- **Say:** "Give me the volume you expect and I'll tell you honestly where it fits."
- **Prove:** Limits from `offerings`, only if stated; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Product, Solutions
- **Status:** seed

## PF-05 Roadmap

- **Heard:** "When will [capability] ship?"
- **Real issue:** A decision gate, or curiosity.
- **Ask:** "Would [capability] change your decision, or is it a nice to have?"
- **Say:** "What is the decision [capability] unlocks? I'll find out what I can share and what I can't."
- **Prove:** Only an approved roadmap statement; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Product; never promise a date
- **Status:** seed

## PF-06 Interface

- **Heard:** "The interface looks hard to use."
- **Real issue:** One bad demo moment, a past tool, or a real usability need.
- **Ask:** "What did you see that worried you?"
- **Say:** "Which step looked hard? Let's do that exact task now."
- **Prove:** `quotes` from similar users; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Demo lead, Customer Success
- **Status:** seed

## PF-07 Simplicity

- **Heard:** "It looks too simple for what we need."
- **Real issue:** A fear of being under-served.
- **Ask:** "What would 'enough' look like for your team?"
- **Say:** "What do you need to do that simple wouldn't cover?"
- **Prove:** `offerings`; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Solutions
- **Status:** seed

## PF-08 Integrations

- **Heard:** "It must connect to [tool]."
- **Real issue:** A must-have, a workaround, or a vague worry.
- **Ask:** "What has to flow between the two, and how often?"
- **Say:** "What data has to move between them, in which direction, how often?"
- **Prove:** Integration list from `offerings`; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Solutions, Product
- **Status:** seed

## PF-09 Speed or performance

- **Heard:** "I heard it's slow."
- **Real issue:** A past experience, a rumour, or a real need.
- **Ask:** "Where did you hear that, and what speed do you need?"
- **Say:** "What would acceptable look like in numbers? Let's test against it."
- **Prove:** A measured, approved benchmark only; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Product, Solutions
- **Status:** seed

## PF-10 Scale

- **Heard:** "Will it work at our size?"
- **Real issue:** A growth worry or a real load.
- **Ask:** "What size are you planning for in two years?"
- **Say:** "What does your size look like now and in two years? I'll show you where customers like that are."
- **Prove:** `quotes`, `approved_metrics`; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Solutions
- **Status:** seed

## PF-11 Local support or language

- **Heard:** "There is no local support in our region or language."
- **Real issue:** A user-facing need or a procurement rule.
- **Ask:** "Which languages, for which users?"
- **Say:** "Who needs it in [language], and what would they use it for?"
- **Prove:** Supported list from `offerings`; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Product; data residency to Security or Legal
- **Status:** seed

## PF-12 Mobile

- **Heard:** "Is there a mobile app?"
- **Real issue:** A frontline need or an executive preference.
- **Ask:** "Who needs it on mobile, and for what?"
- **Say:** "Who would use it on the move, and for which task?"
- **Prove:** `offerings`; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Product
- **Status:** seed

## PF-13 Multi-tenant or multi-entity

- **Heard:** "We run many entities or brands."
- **Real issue:** A structural need.
- **Ask:** "How many entities, and who manages them?"
- **Say:** "Walk me through how your entities are set up today."
- **Prove:** `offerings`; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Solutions, Product
- **Status:** seed

## PF-14 Analytics

- **Heard:** "Reporting looks limited."
- **Real issue:** A specific report or a general worry.
- **Ask:** "Which report or number do you need weekly?"
- **Say:** "Which three numbers would you look at every week?"
- **Prove:** `offerings`; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Solutions
- **Status:** seed

## PF-15 Automation

- **Heard:** "We need more automation."
- **Real issue:** A manual step they hate, or a wish.
- **Ask:** "Which manual step costs you most time today?"
- **Say:** "Which step do you wish ran itself, and how often does it happen?"
- **Prove:** `offerings`; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Product, Solutions
- **Status:** seed

## PF-16 Manual input

- **Heard:** "Too much manual entry."
- **Real issue:** A real workflow or a past bad tool.
- **Ask:** "Which entries, how often, by whom?"
- **Say:** "Show me the entries that annoy you most. Let's see what we can remove."
- **Prove:** `offerings`; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Product, Solutions
- **Status:** seed

## PF-17 Cloud or deployment

- **Heard:** "We can't use a cloud product."
- **Real issue:** A hard policy, a preference, or a misunderstanding.
- **Ask:** "Is that a written policy, and who owns it?"
- **Say:** "Is that a firm policy or a preference? Who can confirm the exact requirement?"
- **Prove:** Deployment options from `offerings`; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Security or Legal, Product
- **Status:** seed

## PF-18 Payment method

- **Heard:** "It doesn't support the payment method we use."
- **Real issue:** A hard operational need, or one team's habit.
- **Ask:** "Which payment methods are required, in which markets, and who decides?"
- **Say:** "Which payment methods do you need, and in which markets? Let me check exactly what is supported."
- **Prove:** Supported methods from `offerings`, only if stated; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Product, Finance
- **Status:** seed

## PF-19 Not industry-specific

- **Heard:** "It isn't built for our industry."
- **Real issue:** A vocabulary gap, a missing reference, or a real regulatory need.
- **Ask:** "What does industry-specific mean for you: terms, workflows, regulation or references?"
- **Say:** "What would make it feel built for your industry: the workflows, the language or the rules?"
- **Prove:** `quotes` and references in their industry, if approved; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** `proof-points`, Solutions
- **Status:** seed
