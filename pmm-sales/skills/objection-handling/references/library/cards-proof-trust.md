# Proof and trust: seed cards

Seed cards. Sourced claims only; if the proof does not exist, say so and offer something checkable.

Every card: **Heard** (the buyer's words), **Real issue**, **Ask**, **Say**, **Prove**, **Route**. Method: `../method.md`.

## PT-01 Unproven vendor

- **Heard:** "We haven't heard of you."
- **Real issue:** Fear of being first.
- **Ask:** "What would make you comfortable?"
- **Say:** "What would you need to see to feel comfortable? A reference in your segment, a trial, a checkable fact?"
- **Prove:** `quotes`, named references if approved; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** `proof-points`
- **Status:** seed

## PT-02 Financial stability

- **Heard:** "Are you going to be around?"
- **Real issue:** A procurement requirement or a worry.
- **Ask:** "Is this a formal requirement or a concern?"
- **Say:** "Is there a specific requirement from Finance or Procurement, or is it a general worry?"
- **Prove:** Only approved statements; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Finance or Legal
- **Status:** seed

## PT-03 Review sites

- **Heard:** "The reviews on [site] say [x]."
- **Real issue:** A real data point or a competitor's plant.
- **Ask:** "What did you read that stuck with you?"
- **Say:** "What stood out? Let me answer that honestly."
- **Prove:** Only approved facts; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Customer Success, `proof-points`
- **Status:** seed

## PT-04 Support quality

- **Heard:** "I've heard support is slow."
- **Real issue:** A past experience or a rumour.
- **Ask:** "Where did you hear that?"
- **Say:** "What kind of support would you need? Let me show you how it works."
- **Prove:** Support facts from `approved_metrics`; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Customer Success
- **Status:** seed

## PT-05 Update stability

- **Heard:** "Frequent changes will disrupt us."
- **Real issue:** A change-management worry.
- **Ask:** "How do you handle change in your team now?"
- **Say:** "How do you handle releases today? Let me explain our approach."
- **Prove:** Release facts, if approved; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Product
- **Status:** seed
