# Security and legal, incumbent, price: seed cards

Seed cards. Security and legal: no claim without a named document. Incumbent: rival claims go to `competitive-battlecard`. Price: see `plays/price.md`.

Every card: **Heard** (the buyer's words), **Real issue**, **Ask**, **Say**, **Prove**, **Route**. Method: `../method.md`.

## SI-01 Security concern

- **Heard:** "Is it secure?"
- **Real issue:** A checklist, a real control, or a past incident.
- **Ask:** "Is there a questionnaire or a specific control?"
- **Say:** "Which controls matter most to you? I'll send the document that answers it."
- **Prove:** A named document only; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Security or Legal owner
- **Status:** seed

## SI-02 Privacy concern

- **Heard:** "What happens with our data?"
- **Real issue:** A privacy review or a regulator.
- **Ask:** "Who reviews privacy on your side, and what do they need?"
- **Say:** "Who owns the privacy review, and what do they need from us?"
- **Prove:** A named document only; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Security or Legal owner
- **Status:** seed

## SI-03 Compliance gap

- **Heard:** "You lack [standard]."
- **Real issue:** A hard requirement or a checkbox.
- **Ask:** "Is it a hard requirement, and who says so?"
- **Say:** "Is that a requirement for this purchase or a preference? Who can confirm?"
- **Prove:** A named document only; never claim what you lack; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Compliance owner
- **Status:** seed

## SI-04 Existing solution works

- **Heard:** "What we have works fine."
- **Real issue:** Habit, sunk cost, or a real fit.
- **Ask:** "What does it cost you to keep it, in time and risk?"
- **Say:** "What does the current setup cost you each month, in people time and risk?"
- **Prove:** `alternatives`, `cost_of_problem`; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** `competitive-battlecard`
- **Status:** seed

## SI-05 Competitor is broader

- **Heard:** "[Rival] does more."
- **Real issue:** A scope comparison.
- **Ask:** "Which of those capabilities matter to you?"
- **Say:** "Which of those would you use in year one?"
- **Prove:** Never quote a rival from memory; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** `competitive-battlecard`
- **Status:** seed

## SI-06 Price too high

- **Heard:** "It's too expensive."
- **Real issue:** One of seven types.
- **Ask:** "Compared to what?"
- **Say:** "Fair question. Let's look at it properly." Then `plays/price.md`.
- **Prove:** `cost_of_problem`, the buyer's numbers; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Deal desk, Finance
- **Status:** seed
