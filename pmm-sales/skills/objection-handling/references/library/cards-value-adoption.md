# Value and adoption: seed cards

Seed cards. The business case is the work; size it in the buyer's numbers before proving anything.

Every card: **Heard** (the buyer's words), **Real issue**, **Ask**, **Say**, **Prove**, **Route**. Method: `../method.md`.

## VA-01 Return unclear

- **Heard:** "I can't see the ROI."
- **Real issue:** The business case was never built.
- **Ask:** "What does the problem cost you today, per month?"
- **Say:** "Let's build it together with your numbers. What does [problem] cost you per [period]?"
- **Prove:** `cost_of_problem`, `approved_metrics`; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** `proof-points`
- **Status:** seed

## VA-02 Adoption worry

- **Heard:** "My team won't use it."
- **Real issue:** A past failed rollout or a real change problem.
- **Ask:** "What happened last time a tool was rolled out?"
- **Say:** "Tell me about the last rollout. What made it stall?"
- **Prove:** `quotes` on adoption; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Customer Success
- **Status:** seed

## VA-03 Looks complex

- **Heard:** "This looks complicated."
- **Real issue:** One screen, one past tool, or a real learning curve.
- **Ask:** "What looked complicated?"
- **Say:** "Which part worried you? Let's walk that one task end to end."
- **Prove:** `quotes`; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Demo lead
- **Status:** seed

## VA-04 Overkill

- **Heard:** "This is more than we need."
- **Real issue:** A scope worry, a price worry in disguise.
- **Ask:** "Which parts would you start with?"
- **Say:** "What is the smallest thing that would be worth doing first?"
- **Prove:** `offerings`; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Deal desk if it is price
- **Status:** seed

## VA-05 Team skills

- **Heard:** "We don't have the skills."
- **Real issue:** A real gap or a fear.
- **Ask:** "Who would own it, and what do they know today?"
- **Say:** "Who would run this day to day, and what support would they want?"
- **Prove:** Onboarding facts from `offerings`; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Customer Success
- **Status:** seed

## VA-06 Free trial or pilot

- **Heard:** "Can we try it first?"
- **Real issue:** A real need to de-risk, or a stall.
- **Ask:** "What would a trial need to show for you to decide?"
- **Say:** "What result would make you say yes at the end of a trial?"
- **Prove:** Approved trial terms only; otherwise "unverified"
- **Route:** Deal desk
- **Status:** seed
