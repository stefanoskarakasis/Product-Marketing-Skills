# Seller card: objection-handling

Pin this in your sales channel. Time to read: 2 minutes.

## What you get
- 🧭 **What the objection really is**, in the thread, in about 100 words.
- 🎯 **The next line to say**, and the one thing not to say.
- ➡️ **A dated next step** with an owner role.
- 🧾 **It logs itself.** You never type `log`. Every answer ends with a one-line receipt and an undo.

## Ask it
- `@Claude they said it's too expensive, it's call two`
- `@Claude digest the Marlow call` (paste or upload the transcript; a connected call tool works once your owner has approved it)
- `@Claude draft a reply to this email` (paste it)
- `@Claude prep me for objections on Northwind`

## Before your first ask
- [ ] Your objection owner has decided whether Claude may read your recorded calls and CRM outcomes (consent, retention and works-council rules apply). Until they approve, Claude only uses what you paste or upload. Not sure? Ask your owner.
- [ ] You are in an internal channel with colleagues only.

## Never
- ❌ Paste a customer's private data, contract terms or contact details into the thread.
- ❌ Send anything the skill wrote. It writes drafts; you decide.
- ❌ Offer a discount because a card suggested it. Cards trade, they do not cut.

## Your own answers
If you have a reply that works, share it. It stays "mine, not cleared" and only you see it until the owner clears it for everyone.

## If the answer looks wrong
Reply in the thread with what is wrong. Your owner sees it and fixes the card.
