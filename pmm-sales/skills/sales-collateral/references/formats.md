# Formats

Read at Step 7. Bold, not italics. Emoji only for a section, an action or a status. Empty sections never print.

## Slack card (at most 150 words)

A bold label with an emoji per section, bullets only, each sentence on its own line.

- 🎯 **The ask:** the asset, audience, stage.
- 📄 **What you get:** where the asset is, in one line.
- ✅ **Sourced:** how many claims, how many named gaps.
- ⚠️ **Before you send:** approval, anything to confirm.
- 📌 **Learned from:** the sibling skills read.
- Ends on one plain question: "Did you send it, and what did the buyer do with it?"

## Full asset

Real headers, sized differently from body text, bullets with one fact per line. Top of the file: asset, audience, stage, date, `built from` (brain sections and sources), confidence, and "needs approval before sending". Gaps are listed at the end as "To confirm".

## Win-loss report

Scope and sources, findings with counts and n, a by-rival table, proposals to each owning skill, what was not read, confidence.

## Routing line

One line: the owning skill, its command, what you will pass along. Never a partial draft after it.
