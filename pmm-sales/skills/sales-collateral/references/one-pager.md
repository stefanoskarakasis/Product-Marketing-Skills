# One-Pager and Leave-Behind

Read for a one-pager, leave-behind or champion summary. A buyer reads it without the rep in the room, so every line must stand alone and be checkable.

## Pick the use

| Use | Reader | Lead with |
|---|---|---|
| Post-meeting recap | Everyone who was on the call | What was agreed, what happens next, by when |
| Champion internal selling | Someone who has to persuade a boss | The case in their boss's words, the risk answered, one number |
| Event or intro handout | A stranger | The problem they recognise, one proof, one next step |

## Structure

1. **The problem** in one sentence, in the buyer's words (`customer_phrases`).
2. **What changes**, plainly, not a feature list.
3. **Up to three differences**, each tied to a source. Unsourced ones are cut or turned into a question.
4. **One proof** from Section 6, with its date. No approved proof: name the gap and show none.
5. **Next step**: one action, one date, one named contact role.

## Rules

- One page, front only. Scannable in 30 seconds.
- A champion version answers the objection their boss will raise first, using the approved `objection-handling` card.
- A comparison to a named rival uses only dated, sourced claims and says where the rival fits better.
- No product jargon the buyer did not use. No invented figure.
- Marked "needs approval before sending".

## Design brief (when someone will lay it out)

For a one-pager, leave-behind, deck outline or ROI layout that goes to a designer or a design tool, end the asset with a brief they can paste anywhere. No design tool or skill is named; it works pasted into whatever the team uses.

1. **Purpose, audience, stage** in one line each.
2. **Format and size:** one page, front only; 16:9 for slides.
3. **Content blocks in order,** each with its final copy and its source tag.
4. **Must not change:** the approved quote, any legal line, and the "needs approval before sending" flag.
5. **Brand inputs:** voice and forbidden phrases from the brain (Section 4). Logo, colours and type are not in the brain: ask once for the brand files, or point to where they live.
6. **Return line:** any wording change goes back to this skill, so the sourcing and the approval check survive; the finished file's link is noted in `_assets.md`.
