# ROI Calculator

Read for an ROI calculator or business case. It is a calculation the buyer can check, not a pitch. Build it as a spreadsheet the seller can change per deal.

## Inputs (the buyer's numbers, not yours)

Time spent on the manual process, current tool cost, error or rework rate, team size, loaded cost per hour. Each input is labelled with where it came from: stated by the buyer, from a call, or a placeholder the rep must replace.

## Calculations

Show every formula in plain words next to the cell. Time saved per period, cost removed, revenue effect only where the buyer gave a baseline. Section 6 `approved_metrics` may set an assumption (for example a customer's measured reduction), cited with its source and date. No benchmark from memory.

## Outputs

Annual return, payback in months, three-year value, each with a conservative, expected and optimistic case. The conservative case leads.

## Rules

- A sample dataset is labelled "example, not a customer".
- Where an input is missing the output says "needs [input]", never an estimate.
- The tab with the assumptions is the first thing the buyer sees.
- Marked "needs approval before sending".
- A designed layout of the calculator uses the design brief in `one-pager.md`.
