# Actions

Read at Step 6. The report earns its place by what the PMM can do on Monday. Three actions at most, each concrete enough to start without a meeting.

## Each action has five parts

**Verb thing.** Why: [one sentence]. Time: [minutes]. Who: [the PMM or a named role]. Evidence: [where the PMM can check].

## What a finding becomes

| Finding | Action | Receiver |
|---|---|---|
| A team with zero uses | Ask the team lead why nobody tried it; offer a 15-minute walk-through | The PMM (a human step) |
| Red companies contacted anyway (3 or more) | Pick three and ask the seller what happened | The PMM |
| A tool at 3 or more companies and not on the rivals list | Add it to the list of rivals | `alternatives-map` |
| A tool that is a known competitor, at 3 or more companies | Raise it for the competitor list | `competitive-profile` (`intake`) |
| A pain named in 3 or more reports and not in your notes | Add it to who-you-sell-to | `product-marketing-context` |
| Companies bigger or smaller than usual at 3 or more | Review the size range | `ideal-customer-profile` (audit) |
| A missing company-notes section that blocked a guess | Fill that section | `product-marketing-context` |
| No CRM answered | Connect the CRM | The admin, see the connector file |

Two findings with the same cause are one action. A finding below its count is a note under "What I could not see", not an action.

## Rules

- Rank by what blocks the most salespeople or the most reports first.
- Never propose a change that conflicts with a value the PMM confirmed; show both values as a question.
- Proposals are shown as exact text with the receiver named. Nothing is written without a yes.
- In an unattended run, write the proposals to the file and send nothing else.
