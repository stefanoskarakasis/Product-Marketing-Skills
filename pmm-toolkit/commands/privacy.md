# /pmm-toolkit:privacy -- Privacy Policy

Draft a privacy policy for a digital product, aware of GDPR, CCPA, UK
GDPR, and other applicable jurisdictions.

## Invocation

```
/pmm-toolkit:privacy Our SaaS product, US and EU customers
/pmm-toolkit:privacy Update our policy for a new mobile app
```

## Workflow

Uses the `privacy-policy` skill. Asks what data is collected and where
users are located, then drafts a policy covering the jurisdictions that
actually apply.
