Mode: cold email. Outbound — the recipient didn't ask for this.
Rule 1: the first line must be about them, not you.
Rule 2: one idea per email. One CTA. Not "let me know if you'd like to chat or learn more or..."
Rule 3: proof over claims. "We helped [similar company] do X" beats "We're the best at X".
For sequences: email 1 = pain hook, email 2 = social proof, email 3 = direct ask. Max 3.
