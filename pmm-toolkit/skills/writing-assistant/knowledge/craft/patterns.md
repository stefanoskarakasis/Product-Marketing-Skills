# Confirmed Messaging Patterns

These patterns have been promoted from hypotheses after 3+ consistent data points.
Each entry records what was observed, not just what is recommended.

Apply these in Mode 3 (Behavioral Messaging Review) Step 3 before drawing from
the general behavioral science reference. Evidence-backed patterns outrank theory.

---

## P-001: Decision-first structure for exec audiences
**Pattern:** Messages to senior leadership that open with the decision or ask — before
any context — are kept without edits more reliably than those that front-load context.
**Applies to:** Slack, email, async updates directed at VP+ or equivalent.
**Signal:** Strong — confirmed across multiple sessions.
**Promoted from:** H-002
**Date confirmed:** 2026-03-18

---

## P-002: Loss framing in B2B subject lines
**Pattern:** Subject lines anchored on what the reader risks losing outperform those
anchored on what they could gain. Effect is stronger when the loss is specific and
near-term ("before Q2 planning locks in") than when it is vague ("don't miss out").
**Applies to:** Outbound email, nurture sequences, re-engagement campaigns.
**Signal:** Medium — confirmed in internal rewrites; not yet tested against live send data.
**Promoted from:** H-001
**Date confirmed:** 2026-03-18
