# Newsletter

A newsletter goes to a list. A message to one person is an email, and it uses the normal
Draft path, not this guide.

## Inputs beyond the shared set

- Who is on the list and why they subscribed.
- The single main point of this issue.
- The sender's name, and any recurring sections the issue should keep.

Default length: 300 to 600 words.

## Structure

1. **Subject line.** Three options. Short enough that a phone does not cut it off. It
   states the value or creates honest curiosity, and the body pays it off.
2. **Preview text.** Adds to the subject line. Does not repeat it.
3. **Opening.** One line on why this issue matters to the reader.
4. **Body.** Two or three blocks. Each starts with a bold first sentence so a skimmer
   still gets the point.
5. **One primary CTA.** One link or button, worded with a verb.
6. **Sign-off** in the sender's voice.
7. **Footer reminder.** Unsubscribe link and sender address. Many regions require them,
   so remind the user to confirm the rules for their regions with their email platform.
   Do not draft legal text.

## Rules

- One main point per issue.
- Write for a phone screen.
- No em dashes in subject lines.
- Numbers and customer names come from the user or the brain, or they are placeholders.
