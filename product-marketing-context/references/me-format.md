# me.md: canonical format

`/foundation/me.md` holds how one person likes to work. It is about you, not the company. The company lives in `/foundation/brain.md`.

## Who may read it, and for what

- Skills may read it for **format and detail only**: how long outputs should be, how much detail, what shape (table, bullets, prose), how to open and close, how hard to push back.
- Skills must never take a company fact from it. A product name, a number or a customer in `me.md` is ignored. If a skill needs that fact, it asks or reads the brain.
- When a request and `me.md` disagree, the request wins for that run.
- Only `product-marketing-context` writes it (Step 9), and only after you confirm the exact before/after.
- Keep it free of private details: no contact details, no health, no personal finances.

## File format

    # How I work
    Last updated: [YYYY-MM-DD]

    role: [your title and level, one line]
    output_length: [short | standard | long]
    detail: [headline first | full reasoning | numbers only]
    shape: [bullets | tables | prose | mixed]
    opening: [lead with the answer | lead with context]
    pushback: [blunt | balanced | gentle]
    review_habit: [how you review drafts, one line]
    avoid: [phrases or habits you dislike, comma separated]
    tools: [categories you use, such as ~~knowledge base, ~~CRM]

## Rules

- All keys are optional. Missing keys mean "use the skill's default".
- Max 12 lines. Short on purpose.
- One person per file. A team keeps one `me.md` per person; this repo does not share it.
- Tags are not needed: everything in this file is stated by you.
